Shadow work, made gentle.

Inspired by Carl Jung, Within turns the moments that trigger you into a way to understand yourself, at your own pace and without judgment.

Reflect on what happened

When something stirs you up, Within invites you to picture the part of you that reacted: give it a shape, hear what it fears, and answer it with care. It is an experience, not a quiz.

A thought is not a fact

Give an overwhelming thought a colour, a texture, a weather and a size, then watch it drift away. Observing a thought creates the space you need to step back from it.

See what keeps coming back

Every moment you log adds to a map of your inner world: the emotions, people and situations that return again and again.

Questions

Is Within free?

Yes. Within is free to download and use on iPhone and Android.

Do I need an account?

No. You only sign in, with Apple or Google, if you want to back up your journal and restore it on a new phone.

Is my journal private?

Your journal is saved on your phone and can be locked with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. We don't sell your data, show ads or use your journal to train AI models. The privacy policy explains exactly what is collected and why.

What is shadow work?

A practice from Jungian psychology: looking at the parts of yourself you tend to push away. With Within you start from a moment that affected you, meet the part that reacted, and notice patterns over time.

Is Within a replacement for therapy?

No. Within is a self-reflection journal, not a medical or therapeutic service. If you are struggling, please reach out to a mental health professional or your local emergency services.

Which languages are available?

English, French and Spanish.
