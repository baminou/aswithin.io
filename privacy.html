Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy explains what information the Within mobile app ("Within", "the app") collects, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. Within is published by Brice-Yazid Aminou, a sole proprietor based in Québec, Canada ("we", "us").

1. The short version

  • Your journal is stored on your phone. You can use Within without an account.
  • If you sign in, your journal is backed up to our cloud database so you can restore it.
  • We use analytics, including screen recordings of app sessions, to understand how Within is used and to improve it. These recordings can include what is shown on screen, including text you type in the app.
  • We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and we don't use your journal to train AI models.

3. Service providers

We rely on the following providers to run Within. They process information on our behalf, under their own security and privacy commitments:

  • Supabase: account sign-in and journal backup (servers in the United States).
  • PostHog: product analytics and session recordings (United States).
  • Mixpanel: product analytics (United States).
  • Sentry: crash and error reporting.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Expo: delivering notifications.
  • RevenueCat, together with Apple and Google: processing and managing subscriptions. Payments are handled by the App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment card details.
  • Apple and Google: sign-in, if you choose to use it.

Because these providers are located outside Québec and Canada, your information may be stored and processed in other countries, including the United States, where local laws may give authorities access to it.

4. What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal information.
  • We don't use your information for advertising, and there are no ads in Within.
  • We don't use your journal to train artificial intelligence models.
  • We share information only with the providers listed above, or when the law requires it.

5. How long we keep information

Journal content on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. Backed-up journal content and account information are kept while your account exists and deleted when you ask us to delete your account. Analytics, session recordings and crash reports are kept for as long as our providers' retention settings allow, generally no more than one year.

6. Your rights and choices

  • Use Within without an account: nothing from your journal is backed up to our cloud database.
  • Notifications: turn them off at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Access, correction and deletion: you can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account and backup by writing to us (see below). We answer within 30 days.
  • Withdraw consent: you can withdraw your consent to a use of your information at any time, which may limit what the app can do.
  • Complaints: if you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or your local data protection authority.

7. Security

Data sent between the app and our providers is encrypted in transit, and access to our systems is limited. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security. If a breach puts you at risk of serious harm, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

8. Children

Within is not intended for children under 14, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Not medical advice

Within is a self-reflection journal, not a medical or mental health service. If you are in crisis, contact your local emergency services.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the date at the top and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.

11. Contact

The person responsible for the protection of personal information is Brice-Yazid Aminou. Write to getwithinapp@gmail.com for any question or request about your information.
